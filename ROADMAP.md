# Open-Source Roadmap

Swift Wellness is committed to releasing the core building blocks of Jolly and Botsolai as open source. This roadmap sets out what we will release and when.

## Phase 1: Foundations (released)

- ✅ **Open Menstrual Pain Log v0.1**: JSON schema for cycle and pain logging, with examples and validation
- ✅ Repository, licensing and contribution guidelines

## Phase 2: Months 1–3

- **Botsolai pain-tracking SDK**: reference implementation for logging, validating and syncing data in the Open Menstrual Pain Log format (Android / Kotlin and JavaScript)
- **Data standard v1.0**: refined with input from the school pilot, clinicians and partner health programmes
- **Privacy and safeguarding guide** for collecting menstrual health data from adolescents

## Phase 3: Months 4–6

- **Jolly Device firmware**: TENS control logic, intensity and safety limits, battery management (Apache 2.0)
- **Jolly Device hardware design files**: schematics, PCB layouts and bill of materials (CERN-OHL-W v2)
- **Model cards** for Botsolai's pain-prediction and anomaly-detection models: training approach, evaluation results, limitations and safeguards

## Phase 4: Months 7–12

- **HMIS integration module**: mapping the data standard to health facility systems, so girls flagged for follow-up can be referred to clinics
- **Anonymised aggregate insights** on menstrual pain trends among African girls, published as open reports
- Application for **Digital Public Goods Alliance** recognition

## What stays closed

To protect user privacy, **individual user data is never published**. Trained model weights built on user data may remain restricted where releasing them could risk re-identification. Model cards will document them openly.
